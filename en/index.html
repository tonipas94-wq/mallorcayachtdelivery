What is Mallorca Yacht Delivery?

Mallorca Yacht Delivery is a yacht delivery service based in Palma de Mallorca. Our professional crew moves your 33–66 ft (10–20 m) motor yacht by sea from one port to another: between ports in Mallorca, usually in a single day, and between Mallorca, Ibiza, Formentera and Menorca. Skipper fees are €250–450 per day depending on length, plus VAT; fuel and berths are paid by the owner at cost. Ibiza → Palma: 74 nm, 4 h 10 min at 18 knots.

FAQ
Straight answers before you ask for a quote

What is Mallorca Yacht Delivery?
Mallorca Yacht Delivery is a boat delivery service based in Palma de Mallorca. Our professionally licensed crew (a skipper and, when needed, a deckhand or marine engineer) takes your motor yacht of 33 to 66 ft (10–20 m) from one port to another: between ports in Mallorca and between Mallorca, Ibiza, Formentera and Menorca.
How much does a yacht delivery in Mallorca cost?
Skipper fees are €250–300 per day for 33–46 ft (10–14 m) and €350–450 per day for 47–66 ft (15–20 m), plus VAT. As the crew is based on the island, a delivery between Mallorca ports is usually done in a single day. For example, Port d'Alcúdia → Palma with a 46 ft planing yacht costs about €405 + VAT, excluding fuel.
How much does it cost to deliver a boat from Ibiza to Mallorca?
Ibiza → Palma is about 74 nautical miles, 4 h 10 min at 18 knots. With a 46 ft yacht and skipper only it takes 2 days of service (travel to Ibiza and the passage) and costs about €770 + VAT. The other way round, Palma → Ibiza, is about €430 + VAT. Fuel is extra.
Do you deliver boats between Menorca and Mallorca?
Yes. Ciutadella → Palma is about 98 nm (5 h 30 min at 18 knots) and Mahón (Maó) → Palma about 113 nm. The shortest crossing is Ciutadella → Port d'Alcúdia, about 37 nm. Ciutadella → Palma with a 46 ft yacht costs about €795 + VAT, excluding fuel.
Do you deliver to or from mainland Spain?
Yes, on request. We focus on deliveries within the Balearics, but we also move boats to or from Barcelona (138 nm to Palma), Valencia (148) or Dénia (134). These are quoted case by case.
Who pays for fuel and berths?
The owner, at cost. Before departure we ask for a float of €1,000–2,500 for fuel and port fees, settled against receipts when the boat is delivered: we refund the balance or invoice the difference.
What happens if the weather is bad on departure day?
We don't leave. The skipper decides with official forecasts; between the islands we watch the tramontana in the Menorca channel and easterly or westerly winds between Ibiza and Mallorca. If the crew is already in port, each standby day is charged at 60% of the daily rate. With flexible dates we choose the window before travelling.
Is my boat insured during the delivery?
Third-party liability insurance is mandatory and fully comprehensive cover is strongly recommended. Check that your policy does not exclude cover when a hired professional is at the helm, and whether your insurer must be notified. We check it with you before confirming.
What qualifications does your crew hold?
Our whole crew is professionally qualified: skippers with the Spanish offshore licence (Patrón de Altura), marine engineers and deckhands, all with valid specialty certificates and medicals. For yachts over 66 ft (20 m) we assign skippers experienced with larger yachts.
Do you deliver foreign-flagged yachts?
Yes. We need the flag state's Certificate of Registry and an insurance policy valid in Spanish waters. The rest of the process is the same.
Can you take my boat to a cargo ship in Palma?
Yes, as an extra: we bring the yacht to the Palma quay where yacht transport ships load, or collect it at discharge and deliver it to your berth.
